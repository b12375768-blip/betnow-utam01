Privacy Policy

Last reviewed: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to your details when you use this website, when you contact the club, and when you come in to play. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past a link.

1. Who we are

Snooker & Billiard is a snooker and billiards club. We are the organisation that runs the tables, the coaching and the club leagues described on this site. Our contact details are on the contact page: telephone [+00 000 000 0000] and email contact@snookerandbilliard.com.

2. What this website collects

Almost nothing, and we want to be precise about that:

  • No account, no login, no newsletter sign-up.There is no profile to create and nothing to sign up for.
  • No cookies set by this website.We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or fingerprinting. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No third-party embeds.The pages use fonts already installed on your device, so visiting a page does not send a request to an external analytics, font or video service.
  • No reading history.We do not know which pages you read, how long you stayed, or where you came from.

Our web host keeps ordinary server access logs — the page requested, the time, and an IP address — for security and to keep the site available. These logs are not used for marketing, are not linked to any account, and are deleted on a short rolling cycle.

3. The enquiry form

The forms on the home page and the contact page work entirely inside your browser. When you press the button, your own email application opens with the message already filled in. You read it, you press send, and the message reaches us through your email provider.

  • The website itself never receives, stores or forwards what you type.
  • There is no database behind the form and no third-party form service.
  • If your email application does not open, nothing is lost: the page also shows our plain email address and a link that reopens the prepared message.

4. Details we hold after you contact us

When you call, email or book, we keep the details needed to run the booking and the club:

  • Your name and contact details (telephone number and/or email address).
  • The booking details: date, start time, duration, table and game.
  • For coaching, the level and focus agreed for the session, so the coach can prepare.
  • For league entries, your name, contact details and match results, so rankings can be kept.
  • Records of any complaint or incident involving your use of the tables.

We use these details only to run the club: to take the booking, to coach you, to arrange fixtures and to contact you about them. We do not sell them, rent them or pass them to advertising networks.

Booking and league records are deleted two years after the last activity, unless you ask us to keep them for longer or a dispute is still open. Records linked to a formal complaint are kept for as long as needed to deal with it and to meet any legal obligation.

5. Cameras and safety

Any CCTV in the club operates under the club’s own signage and notices. We do not stream or publish footage, and we do not use it for marketing.

6. Children and juniors

We do not collect details from anyone under 16 through this website. Junior coaching involves children, so we ask a parent or guardian to make the first enquiry and to provide consent in writing before sessions begin. A parent or guardian remains the contact point for the child’s details. See junior coaching.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • confirm what details we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your details where we are not required to keep them;
  • tell you how we would delete them and how long it would take;
  • object to, or ask us to stop, any use of your details you do not agree with.

Write to us at contact@snookerandbilliard.com. There is no charge for a request and we aim to answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your local data protection regulator.

8. Payments and sensitive details

We never ask for card numbers, passwords, bank details or national identity numbers by email or through this website, and we never ask for payment before a booking is confirmed. If someone claiming to represent the club asks for them, please tell us so that we can warn other players.

9. Security

This website is served over encrypted connections where your browser supports it. Booking and league records are held on club systems that only staff with a need to know can open. No system is perfectly secure, which is why we ask you not to send us details we do not need.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your details, we will update this page and change the review date at the top. If the change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will contact you directly.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy go to contact@snookerandbilliard.com, or by post to Snooker & Billiard, [Street Address], [City], [Postcode].
