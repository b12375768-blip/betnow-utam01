Contact and bookings

For a table that evening, calling is quickest. For anything else — coaching, league entry, an equipment question or a correction to a guide — use the form below or send an email. We aim to reply to written enquiries within two working days.

What happens after you get in touch

  • We read the enquiryA coach or the club manager picks it up, usually the same day during opening hours.
  • We confirm what is possibleIf a table is free we say so and hold it for you. If it is not, we offer the nearest time.
  • We confirm the rateTable hire and coaching fees are quoted at this point and agreed before anything starts.
  • You get it in writingBookings are confirmed by email or text with the table, the start time and the rate, so nobody has to remember what was said.

Common requests

Booking a table

Tell us the game (snooker or pool), the date, the start time and how long you want. We keep booked tables for fifteen minutes past the start time.

Coaching

Tell us your current level in one sentence and what you want to work on. We will match you with a coach and confirm a time. See coaching and leagues for what a session covers.

League entry

Tell us whether you play singles or doubles, and how much you have played recently. Beginners are welcome in the ladder — see the league formats.

Corrections to a guide

Tell us which page and which paragraph is wrong. We will fix it and update the review date.

Accessibility and playing conditions

Tell us what you need — a quieter table, more time between frames, a particular approach to the table — and we will do what we can, or tell you honestly what we cannot do.

Send an enquiry

The form prepares the message in your own email app and opens it for you to review and send. Nothing you type is transmitted to this website or stored by it. If your email app does not open, write to contact@snookerandbilliard.com with the same details.

Before you send

  • We never ask for payment details, card numbers or passwords by email or by this form.
  • We never ask you to send identification to book a table.
  • We will never contact you to ask for a deposit before a booking is confirmed.

If someone claiming to be from this club asks you for any of those things, do not send them, and please tell us through this page so we can warn other players.

Reading our pages

Details of what we do with your message are set out in the Privacy Policy. The rules for playing, booking and using our club are in the Terms & Conditions, including the rule that no money is wagered on our tables.
