About Snooker & Billiard

A cue sports club built around one idea: the game is better when someone will tell you honestly what your problem is. Everything we do — the tables, the coaching, the leagues and these guides — comes back to that.

What we are

Snooker & Billiard is a club, not a website project. People pay for table time, for coaching and, if they want it, to enter a league. The rest of what we do is table maintenance, referee duties and writing the guides on this site.

The club runs full-size snooker tables and 9 ft pool tables, coaching for adults and juniors, and club leagues that run through the year. We hire cues and chalk, we lend bridge rests and gloves, and we fix small cue problems at the counter.

How we work

  • Prices are confirmed when you book.Table hire and coaching rates are agreed at the counter or by phone, because they depend on the day, the time and the table. We would rather tell you the actual rate than publish one that turns out to be wrong.
  • No money changes hands on the tables.There are no cash prizes, no entry stakes and no side bets. Our leagues decide titles, ranking places and table time. Wagering money on a game of skill is not permitted on our tables, and a referee will stop it.
  • Honest assessment.If your cue does not need re-tipping, we will tell you it does not need re-tipping.
  • No pressure selling.Nobody will approach you at a table with a sales pitch. Ask for equipment advice if you want it.

Who writes the guides

Every guide on this site is written by a coach or referee who works at the club, or reviewed by one before it is published. The rules pages follow the current World Snooker rules for snooker and the house rule card used on our pool tables. Each guide carries the date it was last reviewed.

We write about things we have been asked at the counter, and about things that cost players points: break shots, cue ball control, safety play, positioning, table manners and cloth care. We do not write “top 10” listicles, we do not recommend products we have not used on our tables, and we do not accept payment to change what a page says.

What we do not do

  • No paid reviews or sponsored recommendations.
  • No claims about how much better you will play, or how quickly you will improve.
  • No advertising inside the guides and no tracking of what you read.
  • No competitions, codes or offers that require a purchase or a deposit to enter.
  • No content that is shown to search engines but hidden from visitors. This site is exactly what you see in your browser.

Corrections

If a guide contains an error, an outdated rule or an instruction that does not work in practice, tell us through the enquiry form or by email. We correct the page and update the review date. We would rather have an accurate page than a complete one.

Access and playing conditions

If you need a quieter table, longer gaps between frames, extra time to set up, or a table you can approach from a particular side, ask when you book and we will try to arrange it. If there is something we cannot provide on our current tables, we will say so rather than promise it and disappoint you.
