Corrections

Found a mistake?

These guides are written by people who play and coach at the club, which means we get things wrong sometimes. If you find an error in a guide, an outdated rule description, or a drill that does not work as written, tell us and we will fix the page and say what changed.

We also do not publish anything a manufacturer has paid for, and we do not write "best cue" or "must-have" pieces. If a guide recommends equipment, it is because it has been used on our tables.

Suggest a topic

If there is a question you would like answered — a rules situation, a drill, a table problem — send it in. Requests from players are the reason most of these pages exist.
