3. Opening hours and closures

The standard hours are Monday to Thursday 10:00–23:00, Friday 14:00–00:00 and Saturday and Sunday 10:00–00:00. Tables are available on a first-come basis when they are not booked. Maintenance closures, private events and league nights can change what is available, and the notice board at the door always shows the current week.

4. Bookings

  • A booking is confirmed when you receive it from us in writing, by phone, text or email, with the table, the start time and the fee.
  • Fees for table hire and coaching are agreed before the session starts. They depend on the day, the time and the table, which is why they are not listed on this website.
  • Booked tables are held for fifteen minutes past the start time. After that they may be released, particularly at busy times.
  • If you cannot arrive, tell us. Tables left empty at busy times can be given to players who are waiting.
  • Coaching sessions cancelled with more than 24 hours’ notice can be rescheduled without a charge. Later cancellations may be charged in full because the table and coach were held for you.
  • We may refuse or cancel a booking for reasons such as maintenance, an overbooking made in error, or conduct that breaches section 7. If we cancel for club reasons, you are not charged.
  • Please arrive with enough time to change and set up. Time spent setting up is normally part of the booking.

Membership is arranged at the club and is not sold through this website. Membership rates are confirmed in the club, in writing, when you join and at each renewal. You may cancel your membership by giving notice in the form set out in your membership terms; we do not charge a cancellation fee unless that was agreed with you in advance.

6. Equipment we lend

Cues, chalk, bridge rests and gloves can be borrowed. A deposit may be required and is returned when the item comes back. You are responsible for loss or damage of club equipment while it is in your care; ordinary wear from play is not damage.

7. Conduct on the club floor

These are house rules. They exist so that eight tables can run at once without anyone losing the will to play.

  • Play on the table you have been allocated, and ask before moving to another.
  • Chalk on the rail, not on the cloth, and mark the spot where a ball is replaced.
  • Stay still while an opponent is playing. Conversation is welcome, just not during the stroke.
  • Confirm the score with your opponent before the next frame begins.
  • Do not smoke, vape or drink alcohol on the playing floor.
  • Report damage to the table, cloth or equipment to staff straight away.
  • Players under 16 need to be accompanied by an adult who stays on the premises.
  • Abusive behaviour, harassment or deliberate damage will end your booking, and may end your membership.

8. No money is wagered on our tables

This is absolute: there is no cash on the table, no side bets, no entry stakes and no cash prizes, from us or from anyone else. Our leagues decide titles, ranking places and table time only. Placing a wager on a game of skill on our tables is not permitted, and a referee will end the game if it starts. We do not promote, advertise or facilitate any form of gambling, and our website contains no gambling content, no betting offers and no links to betting services.

9. Rules, referees and league play

  • Snooker is played under the current World Snooker rules. Pool is played under the house rule card fixed to each table, which follows the English pool rules unless stated otherwise.
  • A referee’s ruling is final for that frame. If you disagree, raise it before the next shot is played.
  • League formats, handicaps, fixtures and promotion are decided by the league organiser and are published on the notice board and to players on the mailing list.
  • Where a league match is interrupted, the organiser decides how the frame or match is completed.

10. Coaching

Coaching sessions are booked in advance and confirmed with a start time and a fee. The coach will cover the points agreed in advance, but the level of improvement depends on how much you practise between sessions. Juniors require a parent or guardian’s consent, and a first session is arranged with them present.

11. Health and safety

The cue sports we play involve fast-moving objects and long stretches of standing. You are responsible for your own physical condition and should tell staff about any condition that affects your play, including conditions that affect your vision or balance. Wet floors, uneven lighting and damaged equipment are reported to staff immediately. First-aid supplies and emergency contact details are at the counter.
